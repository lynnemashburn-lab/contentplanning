# Trip Content Playbook

**Happily Ever Planned** · How to turn any trip into a content plan: day-by-day call sheets, scripts split into takes, and a posting plan.

Built from the Disney World fall 2026 plan (`disney-fall-2026/`). Everything below worked on that trip and carries over to the next one.

## 1. Gather the trip details first

A plan is only as good as its inputs. Collect these before any scripts get written.

| Detail | Why it matters for content | Where it usually lives |
|---|---|---|
| Dates and the park or activity each day | Shapes every day's call sheet | Itinerary (`trips/<name>.json` in Disney-trip-planner) |
| Resort and room type | Resort tour and "who should book it" videos | Confirmation |
| Who's going, and what you're comfortable showing | On-camera plans and privacy | You |
| Drive or fly | Arrival, checkout and travel-day content | Packing list |
| Dining reservations | Character-meal and food reviews | Itinerary |
| Lightning Lane picks (Multi Pass, Single Pass) | Strategy videos and wait-time proof | Itinerary |
| Events: parties, festivals, holidays | Timely videos with a post-by date | Itinerary |
| What's new or closed that season | "What's new" scripts | Disney Parks Blog, TouringPlans |
| Gear you're bringing | What shots are possible | Packing list |
| Platforms and the goal for this trip | Formats and calls to action | You |

## 2. My rules

**Voice**
- [ ] Warm, professional, Disney-happy but not cheesy
- [ ] No emoji, no AI-sounding filler
- [ ] Short spoken sentences, one idea per take
- [ ] Current Disney terms: Lightning Lane Multi Pass and Single Pass, Early Entry, Extended Evening Hours, ADRs, Park Hopper

**Facts**
- [ ] Every fact comes from the itinerary or a named source
- [ ] Anything only known on the day goes in `[brackets]`: wait times, favorite dishes, verdicts
- [ ] Hours and showtimes change, so check the app each morning

**Signature tips to work in**
- [ ] Be at the resort bus stop two hours before the park's official opening (not Early Entry). Driving? Same rule.

**Privacy and park rules**
- [ ] Crop names, confirmation numbers and ticket or party barcodes out of screenshots
- [ ] No house number or street in travel-day clips
- [ ] Decide which names and faces are okay to share before the trip
- [ ] Tripods are allowed if they fit inside a standard backpack and extend less than 6 ft. No selfie sticks. Phones away on coasters.

## 3. The content mix

Aim for **1–2 must-film scripts and 2–3 bonus scripts per park day**, stories every day, and one reel a day if the family schedule allows.

| Pillar | Example from fall 2026 | When to post |
|---|---|---|
| Expert strategy | My #1 rope-drop rule | Evergreen |
| What's new or closed | Muppets coaster first look · What's new at Animal Kingdom | Right away, then evergreen |
| Seasonal events | 5 things to know before the Halloween party | During the season, before it ends |
| Dining and experiences | Topolino's Terrace review · Food & Wine ratings | During the festival, or evergreen |
| Resort | Treehouse Villa tour · Who should book a Treehouse | While it's news, then evergreen |
| Planning and logistics | Mobile order at 10:30 · Checkout-day tips | Evergreen |
| Lead generation | Before you book this exact week | Now, and again when families book that season |

**Post timely content first.** If an event or festival is still running, those videos jump the line.

## 4. How to write a script

### Anatomy

| Part | What it is | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| Slate | S01, S02… | Numbers stay fixed once checkmarks exist |
| Title and short title | Full name, plus a short one for lists | The short one fits on a phone row |
| Format | Talking head, voiceover + b-roll, screen recording, or quick cuts | Pick what the location allows |
| Where and when | Tied to a moment in the itinerary | "At the bus stop, 6:00a" beats "sometime Monday" |
| On-screen text | The hook as a text overlay | 10 words or fewer |
| Takes | Hook, 2–4 beats, call to action | 1–2 sentences each, recorded one at a time |
| Length | About 2.6 spoken words per second | 30–45 seconds total |
| Caption | 2–4 sentences, the call to action, then 5 hashtags | Keep `[fill-ins]` in the draft |
| B-roll list | The clips the edit needs | Film wide, medium, close |
| When to post | A date or a week | Timely vs. evergreen |

### A complete example

> **S08 · My #1 rope-drop rule** · Must film · Talking head across the morning
> **Film:** Takes 1–2 heading out at 6. Take 3 after Flight of Passage. Takes 4–5 around 10:30.
> **On-screen text:** Why we're heading out at 6 a.m.
>
> 1. **Hook:** "It's 6 a.m. and we're [at the bus stop / already in the car]. Animal Kingdom doesn't open until 8. Here's why."
> 2. "My rule for every client: be at your resort bus stop two hours before the park's official opening. Not Early Entry. Official opening. Driving? Same rule."
> 3. "It's [time], and we've already ridden Flight of Passage. The wait when we got in line was [wait] minutes."
> 4. "Here's the same ride now. [Wait] minutes. That's what the early bus buys you."
> 5. **Call to action:** "Follow me for the rest of my rope-drop rules."
>
> **Proof shots:** Flight of Passage wait screenshots at 7:30 and 10:30.

### Hook patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Time + curiosity | "It's 6 a.m. and we're at the bus stop. Here's why." |
| Contrast | "Same parade. Same spot. 3 hours apart." |
| List | "5 things to know before the Not-So-Scary party" |
| First look | "First look: the new Muppets coaster" |
| Decision | "Should you book a Disney Treehouse Villa?" |
| Lead-in for next year | "Thinking about late September at Disney? Watch this first." |

### Proof beats opinion
Screenshot a wait time early and again later. Film the same spot at two times. Numbers on screen make a tip believable.

### Calls to action

- Follow along this week. I'll show you what worked and what I'd skip.
- Save this for your trip.
- Send me a message if you'd like help planning yours.
- Comment PARTY and I'll send you my full party-night playbook. *(Keep the playbook text ready to paste.)*
- Want this week next year? Send me a message. I'll plan it for you.

## 5. How to build each day

**Every day is a call sheet**: moments in time order, pulled straight from the itinerary.

```
Heading out → Rope drop → Mid-morning → Meal → Rest time → Evening → 10-minute wrap
```

| Item type | What it means |
|---|---|
| Script | Takes to record at this moment |
| Video | B-roll: hold 5 seconds, one move at a time |
| Photo | Stills for carousels and thumbnails |
| Story | Post live, same day |
| Screen | Screenshots or screen recordings from the app |
| Post | Edit and publish a script |
| To do | Anything else: charging, backups, showtimes |

**The night before:** free up 30 GB, set video to 4K at 30 fps, turn on the grid, save three audios, charge and test the mic, pack the tripod, chargers and lens cloth.

**Every night, the 10-minute wrap:** charge everything, heart the day's keepers, write three notes (best moment, one surprise, one tip), and let clips back up on Wi-Fi.

**Rest time is recording time.** Talking heads and voiceovers are easier in the room than in a crowd.

**After the trip:** a posting plan by week. Timely videos first, then rope-drop and strategy, then the evergreen bank.

## 6. Reuse the phone page

The fall 2026 page (`disney-fall-2026/index.html`) is a template. Everything outside the plan data stays the same.

1. Copy `disney-fall-2026/` to a new folder, for example `disney-spring-2027/`.
2. Replace the three lists at the top of the `<script>` block:
   - `DAYS`: each day's moments and items
   - `SCRIPTS`: scripts and their takes
   - `KIT`: tips, playbook, calls to action and hashtags
3. Keep these rules:
   - [ ] Every item and script id is unique
   - [ ] Script items only list take numbers that exist
   - [ ] Every script has exactly one Post item somewhere in the plan
   - [ ] Dates are written `YYYY-MM-DD`
   - [ ] Each day's `park` is one of `prep, travel, party, ak, ep, hs, mk, after` (this sets its color and emblem). A new kind of day, like a cruise or Universal, needs a color and emblem added first.
4. Publish it as a Claude artifact with the `db` capability, so checkmarks back up beyond the phone.

**Already built in:** the day strip with progress bars, Next up, Hide done, type filters, scripts that slide up from the bottom, Take mode (big type, screen stays awake), copy buttons for captions, Filmed / Edited / Posted status, light and dark display, and confetti when a whole day is done.
